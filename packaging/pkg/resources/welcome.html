bacscan

BACnet/IP scanner and network survey for building networks - strictly read-only.

This installs:

  • bacscan.app in Applications (the GUI)
  • the command bacscan in /usr/local/bin (the same app, for the Terminal)

On first use macOS asks whether bacscan may find devices on your local network and accept incoming network connections. Allow both - BACnet devices answer over UDP.
